### Part 1: Daily Itinerary & Sequential Timeline

  * **Hotel Check-In & Base Drop:** Walk *400m* from Palermo Stazione Centrale to **Liberty Palace** (*Via Roma, 72*). Drop bags and check in (*12:45–13:30*).

* **13:30 – 17:00 | Historic Markets, Street Snacks & Quattro Canti Axis**
  * **Main Walking Axis:** Walk North up **Via Roma**, turning West onto **Via Vittorio Emanuele** through the historic heart of Palermo.
  * **Mercato Ballarò Exploration:** Walk through **Mercato di Ballarò** (*Piazza Ballarò / Via Albergheria*; *Hours:* **07:00–19:00**). Palermo's oldest active street market.
  * **Rustic Market Street Food:** **Friggitoria Chiluzzo** (*Piazza Kalsa, 11*; *Hours:* **09:00–20:00**) or market stalls around Ballarò. Grab fresh ***Panino con Panelle e Crocchè*** (*chickpea fritters and potato croquettes in a sesame bun*, *~€2.50*) or ***Sfincione Palermitano*** (*thick spongy pizza topped with tomato, onions, caciocavallo cheese, anchovies, and oregano*, *~€2.50*).
  * **Quattro Canti & Piazza Pretoria Cluster:**
    * **Quattro Canti:** Junction of *Via Vittorio Emanuele* and *Via Maqueda*. Monumental 17th-century octagonal Baroque intersection framed by four symmetrical fountains and facades.
    * **Fontana Pretoria:** *Piazza Pretoria*. Renaissance marble fountain adorned with mythological statues (*"Fountain of Shame"*).
    * **Chiesa di San Cataldo & La Martorana:** *Piazza Bellini* (~*50m* East of Quattro Canti). *San Cataldo* features distinct red Arab-Norman domes (*Hours:* **10:00–17:00**; *Price:* **€2.50**).

* **17:00 – 18:30 | Cathedral Roof Overlook & Natural Gelato**
  * **Cattedrale di Palermo:** *Via Vittorio Emanuele*. Monumental fortress-cathedral exhibiting Arab-Norman, Gothic, and Baroque architecture (*Hours:* **09:30–19:00**; *Price:* **Free** / **€7.00** for roof terraced walk). Walk the upper roof catwalks for panoramic views across Palermo's basin.
  * **Artisan Gelato Stop:** **Cappadonia Gelati** (*Piazza Bologni, 8* or *Corso Vittorio Emanuele, 198*; *Hours:* **10:30–23:00**). Natural artisan gelato featuring local Late Ciaculli mandarin (*Mandarino Tardivo di Ciaculli*) and Bronte pistachio (*~€3.00*).

* **18:30 – Late | Bottle Shopping, Enoteca Aperitivo & Local Dinner**
  * **Spirits Sourcing & Aperitivo:** **Enoteca Picone** (*Via Camillo Benso Cavour, 16*; *Hours:* **16:30–23:30**). Historic wine shop and enoteca (est. 1946). Browse an extensive inventory of western Sicilian spirits and sit for an outdoor spritz paired with a ***Tagliere di Salumi e Formaggi*** with Madonie cheeses and Nebrodi cured meats (*~€16.00*).
  * **Dinner Option A:** **Trattoria Ai Cascinari** (*Via S. Agostino, 37*; *Hours:* **12:30–15:00** & **19:30–23:00**). Classic rustic Palermitano institution. Order ***Anelletti al Forno (Ragù/Carne)*** (*baked ring pasta with ragù, peas, and caciocavallo*, *~€11.00*) or ***Involtini di Carne alla Palermitana*** (*beef rolls stuffed with breadcrumbs, raisins, pine nuts, and bay leaves*, *~€14.00*).
  * **Dinner Option B:** **Osteria Ballarò** (*Via Bolognetti, 3*; *Hours:* **12:30–15:00** & **19:30–23:00**). Set inside the former stables of Palazzo Cattolica. Order ***Pasta con le Sarde e Finocchietto*** (*spaghetti with wild fennel, sardines, pine nuts, and raisins*, *~€13.00*) or ***Stoccafisso alla Palermitana*** (*slow-cooked salt cod with tomatoes, capers, and olives*, *~€16.00*).
  * **Targeted Regional Amari (<35% ABV):** **Amaro Segesta** (*29% ABV, wild herbal maceration from western hills*), **Amaro Monte Polizo** (*30% ABV, citrus & mountain herbs*), and **Amaro Florio** (*32% ABV, citrus peel & herbs*).
  * **Rest:** Return to **Liberty Palace** (*Via Roma, 72*).

## Wednesday, September 30: Day Trip to Trapani & Hilltop Erice Cable Car

### Part 1: Daily Itinerary & Sequential Timeline

* **07:30 – 10:30 | Intercity Bus to Trapani, City Transit & Funicular Ascend**
  * **Palermo to Trapani Intercity Bus Transit Options:** **Segesta Autolinee** coach from *Palermo Stazione Centrale* (*Via Paolo Balsamo*) to *Trapani Porto* (*Via Giuseppe Garibaldi / Piazza Egadi*):
    * Palermo to Trapani (*07:30–09:00*, *€9.60*, *1h 30m*, *Segesta Autolinee*) or (*08:00–09:30*, *€9.60*, *1h 30m*, *Segesta Autolinee*) or (*08:30–10:00*, *€9.60*, *1h 30m*, *Segesta Autolinee*)
  * **Trapani City Bus to Cable Car:** Walk from Trapani Port to *Piazza Vittorio Emanuele* / *Via Fardella* to board local **ATM Bus Line 21 or 23** directly to the cable car base station (*Stazione di Valle Funivia Erice*, *~15m*, *€1.50*).
  * **Funivia Erice Cable Car Ascend:** Board **Funivia Erice** (*Via Capua, Casa Santa*). Panoramic 10-minute cable car ride ascending *750m* up Mount Erice to the medieval hilltop town (*Hours:* **08:30–20:00**; *Price:* **€6.00 one-way / €10.00 round-trip**; *10m*).

* **10:30 – 13:30 | Medieval Erice, Norman Castles & Historic Pastries**
  * **Main Walking Axis:** Enter Erice via **Porta Trapani** and follow **Via Vito Carvini** to **Piazza Umberto I**.
  * **Erice Hilltop Landmarks:**
    * **Duomo di Erice (Real Chiesa Madrice Insigne Salvatore):** Immediately inside Porta Trapani (*Hours:* **10:00–18:00**; *Price:* **€2.50** / **€6.00** combined pass). 14th-century Gothic church with a detached bell tower (*Torre di Federico*).
    * **Castello di Venere & Torrette Pepoli:** Southeastern cliff edge of Erice (*Hours:* **10:00–18:00**; *Price:* **€4.00**). Norman fortress built atop an ancient Temple of Venus with sweeping overlooks of the Trapani salt pans and Egadi Islands.
  * **Historic Pastry Stop:** **Pasticceria Maria Grammatico** (*Via Vittorio Emanuele, 14*; *Hours:* **09:00–20:00**). World-famous pastry shop run by Maria Grammatico. Sample traditional almond paste pastries (*Genovesi Ericine*—warm pastry filled with lemon custard, *~€2.00*) and almond flour biscuits (*Bocconcini di Erice*, *~€1.50*).
  * **Erice Spirits & Pantry Sourcing:** **Maria Grammatico Shop** or **Enoteca Strada del Vino Erice** (*Via Vito Carvini, 12*; *Hours:* **10:00–18:00**). Pick up bottles of regional mountain amari (**Amaro Monte Polizo**, **Amaro Segesta**) and local carob liqueurs.

* **13:30 – 17:00 | Return to Trapani, Salt Pans & Old Town Walk**
  * **Funivia Descent to Trapani:** Board **Funivia Erice** back down to Trapani base station (*10m*). Take **ATM Bus Line 21/23** back to Trapani Old Town (*Piazza Garibaldi*).
  * **Trapani Old Town Walking Axis:** Walk **Corso Vittorio Emanuele** and **Via Torrearsa**.
  * **Trapani Landmarks & Coastal Fort:**
    * **Cattedrale di San Lorenzo:** *Corso Vittorio Emanuele* (*Hours:* **08:30–12:00** & **15:30–19:00**; *Price:* **Free**). Baroque cathedral featuring delicate stuccoes.
    * **Torre di Ligny:** Tip of the Trapani peninsula (*Hours:* **10:00–13:00** & **16:00–19:00**; *Price:* **€1.00**). 17th-century coastal watchtower extending into the sea.
  * **Rustic Trapani Seafood Bite:** **Antichi Sapori Ristorante** (*Corso Vittorio Emanuele, 23*) or street stalls along the harbor. Order authentic Trapanese fish couscous steamed with aromatic broth (*Cuscus di Pesce alla Trapanese*, *~€14.00*) or grilled swordfish sandwiches.

* **17:00 – Late | Trapani Enoteca, Return Bus to Palermo & Dinner**
  * **Trapani Enoteca & Amari Bottle Sourcing:** **Enoteca Garibaldi** (*Via Giuseppe Garibaldi, 45*; *Hours:* **10:00–21:00**). Pick up bottled western Sicilian digestifs (**Amaro Monte Polizo**, **Amaro Segesta**, **Amaro Florio**). Sit for an outdoor glass of local Zibibbo wine or spritz paired with salt-cured tuna heart (*Lattume*) and local cheeses.
  * **Trapani to Palermo Return Intercity Bus Transit Options:** **Segesta Autolinee** coach from *Trapani Porto* (*Piazza Egadi*) to *Palermo Stazione Centrale*:
    * Trapani to Palermo (*18:30–20:00*, *€9.60*, *1h 30m*, *Segesta Autolinee*) or (*19:00–20:30*, *€9.60*, *1h 30m*, *Segesta Autolinee*) or (*20:00–21:30*, *€9.60*, *1h 30m*, *Segesta Autolinee*)
  * **Palermo Dinner Option A:** **Trattoria da Salvo** (*Via Paranza, 10* — near Palermo Port/Kalsa; *Hours:* **19:30–23:00**). Rustic seafood spot. Order fresh grilled catch of the day (*Misto di Pesce alla Griglia*, *~€16.00*) or pasta with red prawns and pistachio (*Pasta con Gambero Rosso e Pistacchio*, *~€15.00*).
  * **Palermo Dinner Option B:** **Trattoria Ferro di Cavallo** (*Via Venezia, 20*; *Hours:* **12:00–15:00** & **19:30–23:00**). Bustling historic center staple. Order ***Calamari Fritti*** (*crispy fried squid*, *~€10.00*) or ***Spaghetti con Nero di Seppia*** (*squid ink pasta*, *~€11.00*).
  * **Rest:** Return to **Liberty Palace** (*Via Roma, 72*).
