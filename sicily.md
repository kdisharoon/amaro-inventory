  * **Base:** Drop bags at **Hotel Trieste** (*Via Maurizio Ascoli, 12* — ~*600m* West of Catania Centrale station, just off *Corso Sicilia*).

* **09:33 – 13:30 | Transit to Siracusa & Mainland Sights**
  * **Catania Centrale to Siracusa Transit Options:**
    * Catania Centrale to Siracusa (*09:33–10:48*, *€8.40*, *1h 15m*, *Trenitalia Regionale*) or (*10:12–11:25*, *€8.40*, *1h 13m*, *Trenitalia Regionale*) or (*11:28–12:38*, *€8.40*, *1h 10m*, *Trenitalia Regionale*)
  * **Mainland Sights (Choose Option A or B):**
    * **Option A:** **Parco Archeologico della Neapolis** (*Mainland Inland Cluster*, *1.2 km* NW of station via *Corso Gelone* / *Viale Augusto*). Ancient 5th-century BC **Teatro Greco**, 2nd-century **Anfiteatro Romano**, and **Orecchio di Dionisio** limestone cave. (*Hours:* **08:30–19:00**, last entry 17:30; *Price:* **€14.00**).
    * **Option B:** **Catacombe di San Giovanni & Borgata District** (*Borgata / Santa Lucia Cluster*, *800m* NE of station via *Via Agrigento*). Subterranean early-Christian tomb labyrinth beneath ruined **Basilica di San Giovanni** (*Piazza San Giovanni, 9*; *Hours:* **09:30–12:30** & **14:30–17:30**; *Price:* **€8.00**). Walk South through the quiet **Borgata** neighborhood down to the coastal promenade.

* **13:45 – 17:30 | Ortigia Exploration, Local Markets & Midday Bites**
  * **Island Arrival Axis:** From Siracusa Station, walk *1 km* Southeast down **Corso Umberto I** across the stone arches of **Ponte Umbertino** onto Ortigia island.
  * **North Market Street Food:** **Mercato di Ortigia** (*Piazza Pancali / Via Cesare Battisti*). Bustling open-air food market. Stop at **Caseificio Borderi** (*Via Cesare Battisti, 82*; *Hours:* **07:00–16:00**, closed Sun) for a handcrafted ***Panino Artigianale*** loaded with warm fresh sheep’s milk ricotta, smoked provola, prosciutto crudo, sun-dried tomatoes, lemon zest, and wild oregano (*~€6.00–€8.00*).
  * **Central Spine Landmarks:** Walk South down **Corso Matteotti** into **Via Saverio Landolina** to arrive at **Piazza del Duomo**.
    * **Duomo di Siracusa:** Center of **Piazza del Duomo**. 7th-century cathedral built around 5th-century BC Greek Doric temple columns. (*Hours:* **09:00–18:30**; *Price:* **€2.00**).
    * **Chiesa di San Martino:** *Via San Martino* (~*150m* West of Piazza Duomo). Tucked-away Gothic chapel featuring early Christian mosaics. (*Hours:* **09:00–17:00**; *Price:* **Free**).
  * **Granita Stop:** **Bar Vogue** (*Via Roma, 68*; *Hours:* **06:30–22:00**) or **Caffè Fiordilatte** (*Corso Matteotti, 16*; *Hours:* **07:00–21:00**) for traditional ***Granita alla Mandorla con Brioche col Tuppo*** (*~€3.50*).
  * **Southwest Waterfront Sight:** **Fonte Aretusa** (~*200m* SW of Piazza Duomo down *Via Picherali*). Natural freshwater spring filled with wild papyrus plants overlooking the sea wall. (*Hours:* Exterior visible **24/7**; *Price:* **Free**).

* **17:30 – 20:00 | Bottle Shopping, Cured Meats, Drink Areas & Targeted Amari**
  * **Market Port Bottle Shop & Aperitivo:** **Bottega Fratelli Burgio Store** (*Piazza Cesare Battisti, 1*; *Hours:* **09:00–21:30**). Dedicated retail shop to buy physical bottles of regional amari, wild capers packed in sea salt, and canned tuna ventresca. Sit at the market counter for a ***Tagliere Misto di Salumi e Formaggi*** (*~€15.00*).
  * **Central Via Roma Corridor:**
    * **Vinosfera** (*Via Roma, 44*; *Hours:* **10:00–20:30**). Specialized wine and craft spirit shop with an extensive shelf inventory of regional amari for bottle purchases.
    * **Ammudda - Salumeria e Cucina** (*Via Roma, 82*; *Hours:* **11:00–23:00**). Casual shop serving regional spritzes alongside a ***Tagliere di Formaggi e Salumi*** with aged Ragusano DOP cheese and Nebrodi prosciutto (*~€12.00–€16.00*).
    * **Enoteca Solaria** (*Via Roma, 86*; *Hours:* **11:00–15:00** & **17:30–23:00**). Atmospheric wine bar specializing in by-the-glass pours of eastern Sicilian digestifs.
  * **Targeted Local Amari (<35% ABV):** **Amaro Amaranca** (*wild orange & Etna herbs, 28% ABV*), **Amaro Indigeno** (*wild pomegranate & herbs, 28% ABV*), **Amaro Sciara** (*artisan Etna botanicals*), and **Amaro Aretusa** (*Siracusan citrus & herbs*).

* **19:30 – 23:00 | Local Dinner Options, Return Transit to Catania & Rest**
  * **Dinner Option A:** **Trattoria La Foglia** (*Via Capodieci, 29*; *Hours:* **12:30–15:00** & **19:30–23:00**). Bohemian Ortigia institution. Order ***Spaghetti con Cozze e Bottarga*** (*handmade pasta with fresh mussels and cured mullet roe*, *~€14.00*) or ***Involtini di Pesce Spada*** (*stuffed swordfish rolls*, *~€16.00*).
  * **Dinner Option B:** **O’ Scina** (*Via del Collegio, 35*; *Hours:* **12:00–15:00** & **19:30–23:00**). Cozy neighborhood trattoria. Order ***Pasta con le Sarde*** (*fresh sardines, wild fennel, pine nuts, and raisins*, *~€13.00*) or ***Polpo alla Luciana*** (*slow-cooked octopus in tomato sauce*, *~€15.00*).
  * **Siracusa to Catania Return Transit Options:**
    * Siracusa to Catania (*18:18–19:33*, *€8.40*, *1h 15m*, *Trenitalia Regionale*) or (*20:10–21:23*, *€8.40*, *1h 13m*, *Trenitalia Regionale*) or (*20:30–21:45*, *€6.20*, *1h 15m*, *Interbus Bus*)
  * **Sleep:** Walk ~*600m* West from Catania Centrale back to **Hotel Trieste** (*Via Maurizio Ascoli, 12*).

## Friday, September 25: Car Rental Pick-Up, Ragusa Ibla & Modica Overnight

### Part 1: Daily Itinerary & Sequential Timeline

* **08:30 – 11:15 | Rental Car Pick-Up & Scenic Drive to Ragusa Ibla**
  * **Car Pick-Up:** Collect vehicle in Catania (**Hertz/Avis** near Catania Centrale station or downtown offices) (*08:30–09:30*).
  * **Drive:** Head **SS194 / SS514** Southwest through the Hyblaean hills directly to Ragusa Ibla (~*105 km* / *1h 45m*).
  * **Parking & Access:** **Parcheggio Repubblica** (*Corso Don Minzoni* — ~*€1.50/hr*). Located at the eastern base of Ragusa Ibla's hill, ~*300m* East of **Piazza Repubblica**.

* **11:15 – 14:00 | Ragusa Ibla Architecture, Streets & Local Bites**
  * **Main Walking Axis:** Enter Ibla from **Piazza Repubblica**, walking East down **Corso XXV Aprile** through the historic center.
  * **Duomo di San Giorgio:** *Piazza Duomo*, Ragusa Ibla. (*Hours:* **10:00–19:00**; *Price:* **Free** / **€3.00** for dome climb). Masterpiece of Sicilian Baroque with a soaring dome and monumental staircase.
  * **Bakery Lunch Stop:** **Panificio DiGerlando** (*Via Carmine, 30* — ~*200m* West of Piazza Duomo; *Hours:* **07:00–14:00**). Grab ***Scaccia Ragusana con Pomodoro e Ragusano*** (*~€2.50*) or ***Scaccia con Ricotta e Salsiccia*** (*~€2.50*).
  * **Cheese Tasting:** **Salumeria Barocco** (*Corso XXV Aprile, 1*; *Hours:* **09:30–20:00**). Sample ***Ragusano DOP Stagionato*** cheese served with local honey.
  * **Eastern Garden Cluster:** **Giardino Ibleo** (*Piazza Odierna* — easternmost tip of Corso XXV Aprile; *Hours:* Open daily until **sunset**; *Price:* **Free**). 19th-century public park with palm avenues and valley overlooks.

* **14:00 – 18:30 | Drive to Modica, Aztec Chocolate & Subterranean Sights**
  * **Drive to Modica:** Head South via **SS115** down into the Modica gorge (~*15 km* / *25 mins*).
  * **Hotel Check-In:** Check in at **B&B Luna Blu** (*Via San Giovanni*) or similar central B&B in Modica Bassa; drop luggage and park nearby.
  * **Main Walking Axis:** **Corso Umberto I** (primary North-South street in Modica Bassa).
  * **Chiesa di San Pietro:** *Corso Umberto I, 15*. (*Hours:* **09:00–19:00**; *Price:* **Free**). Baroque church flanked by a staircase lined with statues of the Twelve Apostles.
  * **Chiesa Rupestre di San Nicolò Inferiore:** *Via San Nicolò* (~*100m* East of Corso Umberto I; *Hours:* **10:00–18:00**; *Price:* **€2.50**). 12th-century rock-cut cave church with Byzantine wall frescoes.
  * **Chocolate & Amari Tasting:** **Antica Dolceria Bonajuto** (*Corso Umberto I, 159*; *Hours:* **09:00–20:00**). Sicily's oldest active chocolate shop (est. 1896). Sample ***Cioccolato di Modica IGP*** (*~€3.00/bar*) and ***Amaro al Cioccolato di Modica***.
  * **Historic Bakery Stop:** **Pasticceria Fede** (*Corso Umberto I, 237*; *Hours:* **07:30–20:30**). Sample ***'Mpanatigghi*** (*beef, almond, and cocoa cookies*, *~€1.50*).
  * **Gelato Stop:** **Gelateria Venti** (*Corso Umberto I, 137*; *Hours:* **11:00–23:00**) for ***Gelato alla Carruba*** (*carob*) and Bronte pistachio (*~€3.00*).
  * **Modica Alta Hilltop Walk:** Walk up stone staircases via *Via De Leva* to **Duomo di San Giorgio** (*Corso San Giorgio*; *Hours:* **09:00–19:00**; *Price:* **Free**), perched above 250 monumental steps.

* **18:30 – Late | Bottle Sourcing, Rustic Dinner & Amari Nightcap**
  * **Bottle Shopping & Aperitivo:** **Enoteca Ragusana** (*Corso Umberto I, 84*; *Hours:* **09:30–13:00** & **16:30–20:30**). Shop for Hyblaean herbal liqueurs and enjoy an outdoor spritz paired with charcuterie.
  * **Dinner Option A:** **Osteria dei Sapori Perduti** (*Corso Umberto I, 228* — Modica Bassa; *Hours:* **12:30–15:00** & **19:30–23:00**). Home-style trattoria. Order ***Costate di Maiale Ripiene*** (*stuffed pork ribs*, *~€15.00*) or ***Ravioli di Ricotta con Sugo di Maiale*** (*~€13.00*).
  * **Dinner Option B:** **Trattoria La Grotta** (*Via San Bartolomeo, 35* — Modica Bassa; *Hours:* **12:30–15:00** & **19:30–23:00**). Cozy trattoria built into stone vaults. Order ***Cavatelli con Macco di Fave e Ricotta*** (*~€11.00*) or ***Coniglio alla Stimpirata*** (*sweet/sour rabbit*, *~€15.00*).
  * **Nightcap:** Walk lower **Corso Umberto I** and stop at **Hemingway Cocktail Bar** (*Largo Piazza Matteotti, 12*; *Hours:* **18:00–02:00**) or **Ristorante / Enoteca Accursio** (*Via Clemente Grimaldi, 39*) for local digestifs.
